Ihsan

Sit with what you feel. Return to what grounds you.

Tell Ihsan what you're carrying today, in your own words or by choosing a feeling, and it finds the verse for you: one verse of the Quran matched to that exact state, a short reflection, one grounded action, and three questions to write into. So you can process, not just cope.

How it works

Four steps, one quiet moment

Runs entirely on your own device. No account and no sign up. Your reflections are saved privately in this browser and never leave it.

01

Say how you feel

Write what's on your mind in your own words, or choose from 26 feelings. Ihsan reads your words and finds the closest one.

02

Find your verse

Every feeling has its own fixed verse, reviewed by hand, with a short reflection, one action and three prompts. Nothing is generated.

03

Write it down

Capture your own thoughts alongside the reflection, in a private running journal.

04

Watch the pattern

Your dashboard shows what you keep feeling and how often you return.

    Backup & privacy

    Everything you write lives in this browser on this device only.

    Back up your journal

    Save a copy of every entry as a file you keep. Do this occasionally, because clearing your browser data will erase entries that have no backup.

    Restore from a backup

    Load a previously downloaded .json backup. Entries are merged with what's already here, and duplicates are skipped.

    Where your reflections are stored

    Ihsan runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no server storing anything. Your entries are saved in this browser's local storage, which means they stay on this device and in this browser. They will not appear if you open the file in a different browser, and clearing site data will remove them.

    Using a shared or public computer? Download a backup when you finish, then use “Erase everything” below.

    Erase everything

    Permanently deletes every entry stored in this browser. This cannot be undone.
